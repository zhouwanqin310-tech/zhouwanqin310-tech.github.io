## Research overview

Using a large parallel comparison corpus and more than one hundred linguistic features, this study applies Biber’s multidimensional analysis framework to compare AI-generated and expert-written academic Chinese.

It examines model choice, register constraints, and prompting. The author-provided summary indicates that larger models or more elaborate prompts do not consistently improve performance, and that semantic averaging occurs across registers.
