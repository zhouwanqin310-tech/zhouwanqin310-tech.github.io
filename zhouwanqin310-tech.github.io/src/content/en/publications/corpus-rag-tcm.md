## Research overview

This study combines domain-specific corpora with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to develop teaching resources for Chinese for Traditional Chinese Medicine.

It examines linguistic accuracy, domain relevance, and pedagogical suitability, focusing on how corpus evidence can guide and constrain generation.
